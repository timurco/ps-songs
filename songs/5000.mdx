## English Translation

“My coming is for all, I shall take up the burden (of
sam’ska’ras) of everyone. To serve all, to honour all, I shall remain with all. Nobody
is distant for me. Everyone’s home is my home. I shall give away completely, love
affection and delight to one and all. Whatever ill may arise for anyone, I shall
resist that. I shall create a basis for peace on earth."
